# Russian music

Most interesting or influential Russian music according to my friends and me.
But actually It's just whatever we could remember.

Ordered by how I felt like

Ukraine flags are links to artist's statements in support of Ukraine or to their charity shows, proving that they're against the invasion.

The artists without the flag probably haven't spoken out about the war
or at least I'm not aware of it.
Most of them still perform in Russia, so public statements against the war
would likely get them 10+ years in prison unless they leave Russia
forever before making such statement.

If you know that any of the listed artists support Putin or the invasion then 
please send me a message to me@unmb.pw and I'll remove them.
