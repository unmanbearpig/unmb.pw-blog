Let's see what happens when I use my action camera to catch fish in a Patagonian river.

Here are the results of my experiment:

1. Turns out DJI makes bad fishing bait, didn't catch anything.
2. DJI Action 4 is waterproof as advertised.
3. Don't put anything between the camera and the case, it made it unlock and I almost lost the camera, haha.

I'll find a better way to mount the camera next time and repeat the experiment in other rivers, seems fun!
