# I like software most when it has to survive contact with reality.

I am a senior backend and product engineer with more than 15 years of professional experience. I build systems where the interesting work sits behind the interface: business rules, changing source data, awkward exceptions, performance, deployment and the question of who remains responsible when something breaks.

My commercial base is Ruby on Rails and PostgreSQL. I spent eight years working with one Rails client, improved high-traffic marketplace pages by roughly 10x, and built extraction systems for hundreds of regulatory sources. Current work adds Go, Python, PostGIS, data acquisition and evaluated AI pipelines to that base.

The projects I care about tend to cross role boundaries. HostelPunk is a live product I designed, built and operate across ingestion, entity resolution, analysis, ranking, interface and infrastructure. For Vestuario Antiguo, I created the visual identity and logo, designed the public experience, built the Rails operating system and continue to manage the technical product. I am comfortable moving between product judgment and implementation because the two usually fail together.

I use coding agents extensively to increase throughput. I define the work, make the product and architecture decisions, review the output, test it, deploy it and remain responsible for production behavior. The leverage is real, but so is the need for someone who can recognize a plausible wrong answer.

I am self-taught and still enjoy learning from first principles. That has taken me from Rails internals and PostgreSQL query plans to Rust utilities, a Linux kernel patch, browser extensions, audio and language tooling, photography and the social consequences of technology. The common thread is curiosity backed by a need to make the result work.

I am based in Santiago, Chile, work fluently in English and overlap with teams across the Americas.
